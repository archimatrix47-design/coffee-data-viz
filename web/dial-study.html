One dial, drawn large

This page renders a single cell through funnel.js and dialcell.js, the same two modules the breakdown grid and the poster call. Nothing is drawn separately here, so whatever this page shows is what the other two will show at their own sizes. Get this one right and the rest follow.

Radius does not change the arc, and should not: it is a size channel, carrying the buyer's volume in the grid, and if it moved the angle as well then no two dials would have comparable shapes. The angle is the arc opening slider, which is the line's offset from the centre stated in degrees. Everything except the type is a fraction of R, so radius and label size are held apart too; tied together, the radius slider is a zoom and nothing changes shape.

Side by side hides small errors in the arc sweep, because the eye cannot hold an angle across a gap. Stacked, a wrong sweep is obvious. Line the reference up with the sliders once, then read the mismatch directly.
