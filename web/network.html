Circle colour is the share of imports in everything a country handles; circle size is the volume it moves.

Click a country and every partner it trades with turns into a small ring showing how much of that partner the selection accounts for. Select an exporter and each buyer fills in by the share of its own intake that came from the selection; select a buyer and each origin fills in by the share of its own shipments that go there. Each ring keeps its own country’s colour from the ramp above, at full strength for the share and ghosted for the rest, and ring size still means volume moved, so the new reading is the angle alone.

This is not the same ranking as the flows. Brazil’s largest route by tonnage is Germany at 42%, but its largest by dependence is Argentina at 90%, and a line on a map can only ever show the first of those.

The selected country itself opens into a ring of where its own coffee came from, one wedge per origin. A country with no recorded intake gets no ring of its own; the flows already show where it ships.
